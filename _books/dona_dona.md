*Dona Dona* is a novel by **Toshikazu Kawaguchi**, the author of the *Before the Coffee Gets Cold* series.  
I just purchased this book recently, and I’m excited to begin reading it.  

From what I’ve heard, the story continues the emotional themes of time, memory, and the hope of being able to say what once remained unspoken.

### 💭 Thoughts & Expectations
I’m looking forward to discovering the characters’ journeys and seeing how this book explores moments we wish we could revisit.  
For now, I’m simply curious and eager to see what lessons and feelings this book will bring.
