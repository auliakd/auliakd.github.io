*No Longer Human* is a classic psychological novel by the Japanese author *Osamu Dazai*.

It follows the story of a young man named Yozo Oba, who struggles with a deep sense of alienation, feeling entirely incapable of understanding other human beings and fitting into society.

I'm currently reading this book. I love how the story feels raw and intensely honest, capturing the painful reality of mental isolation.The author’s words are deeply moving, like a quiet window into a mind that feels completely detached from the rest of the world.

---
###💭 Current Reflections
- The "clown" persona shows how far someone will go to hide their pain just to feel accepted.
- The theme of alienation is heavy: it explores what happens when you feel like an outsider in your own life.
- This book reminds me that sometimes, the hardest battle a person faces is the one happening entirely inside their own head.
