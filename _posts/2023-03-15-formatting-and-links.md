Stepping into Singapore for the first time was more than just a trip; it was an observation of order and innovation. I remember checking in at **Spacepod @ Lavender**, reflecting on the quiet ambition that brought me here. 

I told myself: *"Grow — slowly, softly, but continuously."* Little did I know how much this visit would accelerate that growth.

### **Day 2 — The Turning Point: NUS ISA Research Labs**

<div style="float:left; width:40%; margin: 0 1.5rem 1.5rem 0;">
  <img src="/assets/img/photo12.png" style="width:100%; border-radius:8px;">
</div> 

The highlight of this journey was visiting **NTU** and the **National University of Singapore (NUS)**. 

Beyond the stunning architecture, we had the invaluable opportunity to be guided through the **ISA Research Labs** by a **PhD student**. Hearing firsthand about the high-impact research being conducted there was a "lightbulb moment" for me. It was the first time I saw how Information Systems could bridge the gap between complex data and real-world industrial solutions.

Of course, the journey had its challenges—we got lost for two hours trying to find the campus! But as we say in Indonesia:

> **"Malu bertanya sesat di jalan."**

We asked, we learned, and we eventually arrived. That day, I didn't just find a building; I found my future research interest.

### **Reflection: From 2023 to 2026**

<div style="float:right; width:40%; margin: 0 0 1.5rem 1.5rem;">
  <img src="/assets/img/photo11.jpeg" style="width:100%; border-radius:8px;">

Standing by the water at Gardens by the Bay that evening, I felt a new sense of purpose. I saw a version of myself that was more confident and open to global collaboration.

Fast forward to today: that inspiration has materialized into my current work on **"Intelligent PMIS."** The problems I observed in the industry during my time as a PPC—specifically reporting latency and unstructured data—are exactly the challenges I am now eager to solve back at the place where it all started.

### **A Goal for the Future**
I am returning to these memories today with a clear vision. To the version of me that was inspired in 2023: 

> *"Thank you for the courage to dream. Now, it's time to contribute."*
