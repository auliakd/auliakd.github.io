In late 2023, I conducted an academic visit to the **National University of Singapore (NUS)**, specifically targeting the **Department of Information Systems and Analytics (DISA)**. The primary objective was to observe and learn from the research environment and infrastructure within the **Information Systems and Analytics Research Labs**.
During the session, I was guided by a **PhD researcher** who provided an overview of the lab’s research activities and environment. The discussion focused on several key areas:
- **Research Integration:** How Information Systems research connects theoretical perspectives with practical problems.
- **Collaborative Ecosystem:** How doctoral researchers and faculty members interact within a research-oriented environment.
- **Data-Driven Innovation:** How analytics and information systems research can contribute to organizational and technological development.

### **Developing Academic Resilience**

Visiting a leading academic institution in Asia gave me a valuable perspective on **academic resilience**. The visit also reminded me of the importance of adaptability and the ability to engage in academic discussions across diverse backgrounds.
This experience reinforced the importance of proactive inquiry and persistence—qualities that are essential for any researcher striving to contribute to the international scientific community.

---

### **Alignment with Research Objectives**
The visit to the **Information Systems and Analytics Research Labs** provided important perspectives on how technology can align with organizational goals. This experience directed my research interests toward exploring **Digital Transformation in Organizations and Digital Knowledge Management (DKM)**.

Although I began my research exploration by studying technical capabilities such as AI and NLP, my focus has now shifted toward a more strategic direction. I want to understand how these technologies are actually implemented to support organizational success. Through the **Systematic Literature Review (SLR)** method, I seek to bridge the potential of technology with practical management needs—an approach that aligns with my background in Information Systems.

---

### **Conclusion**

This visit became an important reference point in my academic and professional development. It continues to shape how I think about bridging academic theory with practical challenges in industry.
