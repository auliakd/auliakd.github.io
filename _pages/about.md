I am an Information Systems researcher interested in the intersection of Artificial Intelligence (AI), Digital Knowledge Management (DKM), Natural Language Processing (NLP), and Large Language Models (LLMs). My research explores how these technologies can support knowledge sharing, organizational learning, and data-driven decision-making.

I hold a Bachelor's degree in Information Systems from UIN Sultan Syarif Kasim Riau, Indonesia, where I graduated in 2025. My research experience includes systematic literature reviews, bibliometric analysis, and empirical investigations of digital knowledge-sharing environments. I have also contributed to research on artificial intelligence, project management, and digital knowledge management.

Alongside my academic interests, I work as a Project Planning and Control (PPC) Administrator in the engineering industry, supporting project reporting, documentation, and data management. This professional experience provides practical perspectives on how information and knowledge are managed within project-based organizations.

My current research focuses on developing AI-assisted approaches for retrieving, organizing, synthesizing, and transforming digital information into reusable organizational knowledge. I am particularly interested in knowledge management systems, AI-driven recommender systems, and human-centered decision support, with an emphasis on keeping human judgment central to the decision-making process.
